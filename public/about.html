About Us

Built On 20+ Years In The Field

Concretum Operis Ready Mix is the dedicated concrete supply and delivery division of Concretum Operis, formed to bring the same quality control our crews demand on-site to every yard we deliver.

Who We Are

A concrete contractor's ready mix division

Concretum Operis has placed concrete, handled excavation and performed drilling across Richmond, CA and the East Bay for more than 20 years. Ready Mix was built as a specialized division of that company, focused entirely on batching and delivering concrete.

"Mix designs are checked against the same ACI standards our own crews expect, drivers are briefed on access and pour sequencing before they leave the yard, and every load is tracked from batch to placement."
