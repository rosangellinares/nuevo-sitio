Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

01

Information Collection and Use

We collect your mobile phone number only when you explicitly provide it to us through our website contact forms or direct inquiries. This information is used solely to provide the services you requested, such as:

  • Sending project estimates and proposals.
  • Scheduling site visits and appointments.
  • Providing project updates and notifications.

02

Mobile Information Protection

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

03

Opt-In and Consent

By providing your phone number and checking the consent box on our forms, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing and transactional text messages from Concretum Operis, Inc.

✓ Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

04

Your Choices (Opt-Out)

You're in control. You can cancel the SMS service at any time.

  1. Step 1 Text "STOP" to our phone number.
  2. Step 2 You will receive a confirmation message that you have been unsubscribed.
  3. Step 3 After that, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us.

Want to join again? Simply sign up again the same way you did the first time.

05

Support

We're here to help. If you're experiencing any issues with our messaging program, you have two easy options:

  • Reply with the keyword "HELP" for more assistance.
  • Get help directly on our website: concretumoperis.com

Our team is ready to assist you and ensure you have the best experience with Concretum Operis, Inc.
